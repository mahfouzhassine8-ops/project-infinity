New exports distinguish actual Kodi-resolved component paths from merely present disk copies, report hashes and dependencies, and include filtered touch records.

Providers retain verified supplied-query interfaces and playback routes; unsupported or absent interfaces are clearly unavailable. Light, Dark and OLED use the same controls.
