# Cobra 2103201 — focused device acceptance

Record PASS / FAIL / NOT TESTED with device/OS, installed version, source/channel, Fold screen, orientation, theme and action time. Keep existing application data. This checklist supplements the complete 2103200 acceptance checklist, which still applies to inherited behavior. No physical result is prefilled as a pass.

1. Install over the current signed app and confirm sources, preferences, profiles and recordings remain. Confirm version 2103201. Do not uninstall or clear data.
2. On a live channel, let local history become READY. Press and hold the existing blue timeline past the long-press timeout, drag and release. It must never open Live TV. Confirm the real picture/audio seek, not just movement of the thumb. Repeat near both ends and while paused.
3. Repeat timeline holds while history is warming, on a non-seekable stream and with Live TV Rewind Off. No drawer should open and no unsupported seek should occur. A long-press on the video background should retain its established drawer action.
4. Cancel a drag by leaving the control/context, rotate during a drag, and change channels during pending rewind entry. An old gesture must not seek the replacement player or resume a deliberately paused player. Repeat mini/fullscreen handoff while rewound. Test rewind, fast-forward, pause/resume and LIVE afterward.
5. With a D-pad, move the timeline after the controls have been visible for a few seconds. The controls should remain available for the normal renewed timeout. Check focus and disabled rewind/LIVE/last-channel indicators.
6. On a stream with confirmed English subtitles, select its actual English track in Audio & subtitles and verify visible cues. Turn subtitles Off and confirm the current cue disappears immediately. Switch on again, return to preview and fullscreen, and confirm the CC state agrees. Repeat with a saved channel default of Off; a session override must not silently rewrite that saved default.
7. On a stream without supplied captions, confirm the menu explicitly reports no subtitle tracks. Saving Preferred subtitles → English should remain a clearly labeled preference and must not falsely report a supplied active English track. If tracks appear late, keep the chooser open and confirm it refreshes without losing focus or accepting stale selections. New rows must be fully visible when space permits; long lists must stay within the usable screen and scroll to the last track. Repeat track removal and rediscovery without reopening the menu.
8. Confirm Options has no repeated Aspect row and its other actions remain. Use the dedicated Display button to reach every aspect mode, custom sizing and channel defaults/reset. Confirm all unique channel audio/subtitle preferences remain accessible.
9. Select Fold Adaptive using a video with known circles, grid and edge markers. Check inner/cover portrait/landscape, rotation, multi-window, drawer open/close and PiP return. All source edges must remain visible, centered and proportional in the actual usable video pane. Bars caused by different ratios are expected; stale geometry, stretch and crop are failures. Repeat the other 12 modes against their established behavior.
10. Rapidly show/hide controls, open/close Options and the channel drawer, focus controls during entry, start dragging during a fade, and enter/exit PiP. Controls must not remain shifted, faded, invisible or unresponsive. Disable system animations and repeat. Video playback must remain stable throughout.
11. Check Light, Dark, OLED and Follow System on both displays, including large text and D-pad. Record any clipping, unreadable labels, misplaced sheets or changes in custom-theme appearance.
12. Repeat at least ten mini/fullscreen/PiP transitions and ten channel changes, then play for at least 30 minutes. Compare reference and problem channels on the same network path; export diagnostics immediately after a failure with action time and a short recording when visible behavior is involved.

Any regression in working playback, rewind, source management, themes, navigation, subtitle behavior or data retention blocks locking. Preserve the rollback package and use supported restoration paths only.
