Infinity 2103316 — Close Progress and Cobra Isolation test candidate

Locked parent remains APK 2103312 / skin 1.0.5.201. This candidate retains
2103315's tested Android task-removal close route. Install as an update over
your current candidate; do not uninstall or clear data.

1. Open Infinity/Kodi, choose Power > Close Kodi, and return to Choose Your
   Experience immediately. Watch the blue Infinity gear arc: it should keep
   moving while native cleanup runs, including the observed roughly one-minute
   case. This is a busy indicator, not a percentage or countdown.
2. Before Kodi finishes, tap Cobra. Cobra must open immediately without the
   Kodi-closing warning. Its settings gear, controls, playback and normal Exit
   action should behave as before. Exit Cobra and return to the chooser while
   Kodi is still closing; the Infinity indicator should still reflect Kodi.
3. Keep the chooser visible through a complete close cycle. The Infinity ring
   must become a still full circle only after that same Kodi PID ends with its
   matching native cleanup receipt. Infinity should then reopen normally.
4. Repeat after rotation, background/foreground and Samsung pop-up resizing.
   The arc should resume when visible. With Android animations disabled, a
   static busy arc is intentional; completion must still await real evidence.
5. Set Cobra as the remembered default, close Kodi, and launch the app again.
   Cobra must open without waiting for Kodi preparation or shutdown. Restore
   Ask every time afterward if that is your preference.
6. Test Cobra alone with no Kodi session: launch, settings, playback, and Exit
   should remain unchanged. The Cobra gear must never show a close ring.

If a path fails, export Health Center after the event and note the action/time.
Preserve the existing data and profile. A fresh chooser opened after a completed
close uses the ordinary Infinity gear; it does not replay an old completion ring.

Native engine, all other native libraries, skin, bundled assets/resources,
manifest/process declarations, permanent signer and Cobra player/close classes
are preserved. This candidate makes no claim to shorten native cleanup.
Do not lock or call this device-verified until both experience paths pass on-device.
