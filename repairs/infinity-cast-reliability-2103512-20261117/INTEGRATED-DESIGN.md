# Cast reliability architecture — signed512 foundation

The owner's Onn and Nest Hub Max failures remain separate acceptance failures. The retained native test reproduces both planner rejection before I/O and generic fragment-flush failure on the same valid24-second coded packets when legitimate cue spacing increases from4 to8/12 seconds. These are reproductions of supported-source mechanisms, not identification of the owner's exact packet contents.

Implement together, then verify as one system:

- Keep the128MiB whole-request origin-ingress ceiling,15-second native operation deadline, packet limits and malformed-input/DRM/TLS/authentication checks. A32MiB source byte cache reuses only validated immutable response bytes. Without a strong validator, cache validity ends at reconnect and the separate live response cursor must never be confused with cached replay. Cached replay has its own512MiB hard bound; neither bound resets on seek. Report physical ingress, cache hits and logical reads independently. Saturate a conservative admission estimate instead of treating repeated overlap as proven ingress; actual ingress remains enforced.
- Replace whole-fragment JNI arrays in production with bounded64KiB chunks into a RAM-only immutable resource. Total session output pages, including unpublished and client-pinned pages, are capped at64MiB. No disk or profile writes. Pre-account packet payloads and interleaving before FFmpeg; use bounded multi-moof output instead of allowing movenc to accumulate an unlimited GOP. Preserve every video/AAC packet and timestamp. Assign disjoint request-order-independent mfhd ranges and enforce their maximum count. Publish only a complete, validated representation. Direct regeneration must match its recorded digest; hardware retains whole200 responses, never partial splicing of regenerated encodings.
- Own native/codec/GPU production on one bounded Cast executor. HTTP serving waits outside the owner. Cancellation immediately wakes I/O/GPU and retires queued work; cleanup remains on the owner. Retain pending phone-handoff ownership while reporting success only after owned receiver PLAYING.
- Prebuffer unique contiguous coverage around the actual transfer/seek position, not just movie start. Use one finite startup deadline. Keep codec active/sustained and thermal protections. Assess the90% integrated delivery requirement over a real contiguous readiness window rather than treating a single cold two-second cue as sustained throughput; do not count cache hits or repeated requests as new media coverage. Failure restores the phone. No buffer can hide sustained underproduction.
- Measure AAC, source access, video/codec setup, mux/flush, release and complete generation separately. Continuous codec reuse was evaluated: persistent streaming would need carried RADL pictures, exact encoder IDRs and new discontinuity ownership. The physical332ms video-stage sample does not prove that recreation dominates. Preserve the verified per-fragment codec correctness until phase measurements justify that additional change.

Gate the consolidated candidate on representative high-bitrate/sparse-cue and long-duration output, HTTP/ranges/authentication, AAC/video/timestamp continuity, target seeks, pause/resume, cancellation/phone handoff, bounded resources, actual ARM64/JNI/Android integration, and independent preservation review. Reuse Kodi unchanged. Emulator correctness is never physical Fold throughput or TV acceptance. No intermediate signed APK.
