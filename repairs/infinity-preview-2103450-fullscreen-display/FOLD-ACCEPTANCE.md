# Preview fullscreen/Display physical Fold acceptance

Install the signed testing candidate over locked450; never uninstall or clear
data. Keep the signed450 recovery download. No automatic acceptance or lock.

1. Open a familiar cover-display clip. Enter Fullscreen from the horizontal rail
   between Display and Auto rotate. The video fills the allowed viewport by
   cropping excess edges without stretching, with header/rail/system bars hidden
   as Android permits. Tap video: controls overlay the full-size picture.
2. Exit through Exit fullscreen or Back. The previous Display choice returns,
   including Best Fit or intentionally selected Stretch; movie position and
   audio continue. A later normal Back/Close/Stop returns to the source app.
3. Repeat in portrait and landscape on cover and inner displays. Rotation is
   independent; switching orientation retains the active choice and current
   movie. Check genuine fold/unfold, cutout/gesture areas and window resizing.
4. In normal Display, Best Fit shows the entire picture proportionately; Fill
   crops explicitly; Stretch distorts only when deliberately selected. Open the
   panel repeatedly, check its radio selection, descriptions, Appearance and Done
   in System/Light/Dark. Confirm all rail buttons remain reachable by swiping.
5. Check timeline, ±10s, pause/play, mute and inline volume, Audio/Subtitles,
   Auto/Locked feedback, Resume/Start Over, call-time mute/manual Unmute, and
   existing background/source-return behavior. Android/carrier call policy can
   still enforce silence. All existing limits remain unchanged.
6. Compare with the actual approved mockup and supplied COVER recording. The
   coding environment could read the written design but not those original media
   bytes; no exact visual-match claim is made. Emulator sizes are not physical
   Fold acceptance. Main Infinity/Cobra/native/shutdown payload stays frozen;
   no new shutdown test is selected.
