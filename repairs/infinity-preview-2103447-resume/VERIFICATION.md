## Acceptance limits

API37.1 emulator decoding and synthetic provider evidence do not establish Samsung Fold hardware behavior, proprietary file-manager/Drive account compatibility, every codec, or immediate launcher behavior. No such acceptance is claimed. The original signed dark UI is preserved under Android system theme changes; the separate cinematic Light/Dark design remains unimplemented and locked for its own task.

History retains 1,000 recent videos and 4,000 aliases. Arbitrary cloud copies and opaque identities cannot be safely merged. Abrupt process/power loss may lose the last roughly 10-second checkpoint interval; format/decoder seek granularity still applies. See IMPLEMENTATION.md and FOLD-ACCEPTANCE.md for exact behavior and the physical test steps.
