# Cobra phone glass polish — 2103265 test candidate

Start from your current Infinity installation. Install as an update; keep app data.

Check Mobile, TV Grid, Compact and Cards in OLED and Light: panels, filters,
selected channels and controls should have matching glass sheen and cyan rims.
Open View: all five layout cards should be present and Current view obvious.
Open drawer: Search, Live TV, Movies, Shows, Recordings, My List, View, Settings
and Power remain available; selected navigation should match the active section.

Pro has only styling isolation and LIVE text repair. Choose a loaded live channel:
LIVE must show red letters inside the cyan glass capsule, including after changing
appearance, leaving/returning to Pro, and fold/orientation changes.
Preview, Unmute, source swipe, subtitles, favorite and fullscreen should behave
as before. Non-live sources must not show LIVE.

Check continuous playback while changing modes, opening/closing drawer and View,
PiP/background return, timeshift/rewind, and the removed full-screen hold menu.
Animations should feel smooth and finish promptly. Turn Android animator duration
off: transitions should settle immediately without hidden controls.

Automated Android view tests use deterministic fixtures; they do not prove physical
Fold playback continuity. This candidate is not locked until your device acceptance.
